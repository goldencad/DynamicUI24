# Form and parameter-layout guidance

Normative authority: [v0.16 §10](../specification/DynamicUI24-Spec-v0.16.md#10-universal-editors-and-forms).

Compose related semantic fields into groups, apply readable width, keep compact values compact, and let the framework reflow groups. Do not encode absolute X/Y positions. Keep labels, help, and validation programmatically associated with their editor. `DateRange` is one semantic group and may wrap only as a group.
